In November 2025, I partnered with [Sophie Lülsdorf, Managing Director at Aurevo Energy](https://www.aurevo-energy.com/), stepping into a high-pressure recovery scenario: a broken Prophix implementation, low system trust and a hard deadline—onboard a new customer within 4 months while establishing a scalable data foundation.

## The Challenge

The setup lacked structure, ownership and reliability. Previous implementation attempts had failed, resulting in low adoption and operational friction. At the same time, the business required a fast turnaround, clean data flows and a system capable of supporting growth.

## The Approach

Applying a hypothesis-driven, root-cause analysis approach I focused on rapid stabilization and long-term scalability:

1. Identified and resolved critical system blockers within weeks.

2. Re-architected data flows and system structure for clarity and reliability.

3. Implemented best practices across data lifecycle and process design.

4. Delivered targeted, hands-on training to enable internal ownership.

**The focus:** move from a fragile, consultant-dependent setup to a self-sufficient system.

## The Outcome

Within weeks, the system was stabilized and fully operational. Within 4 months, a new customer was successfully onboarded on schedule. More importantly, the team transitioned to independent system ownership—managing, maintaining and evolving the setup without external reliance.

What started as a recovery project became a scalable, production-ready system and a shift from dependency to capability—enabling sustainable growth built on trust, clarity and execution.
